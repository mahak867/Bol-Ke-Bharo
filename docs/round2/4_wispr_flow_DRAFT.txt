Using Flow ourselves shaped the product. Speaking was faster than typing, but it also showed us what voice input gets wrong: names spelled differently, numbers heard as words, and filler words like "mera naam ... hai". That is why Bol Ke Bharo strips filler words, normalises spoken numbers ("तीस हजार" becomes 30000), shows the exact words each value came from, and never trusts a value without a spoken read-back. Wispr made the keyboard optional for us; we built the same thing for the people who fill India's forms for everyone else.
